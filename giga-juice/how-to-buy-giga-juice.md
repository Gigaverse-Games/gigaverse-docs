# How To Buy Giga Juice?

Giga Juice empowers players with a number of important [benefits](https://app.gitbook.com/o/9E9eJ3Ahdd7XiSDckopG/s/xqDXBs0OB0XkJzPtPE3t/~/changes/22/giga-juice/benefits-of-giga-juice) and is recommended for all players looking for the best way to play & experience Gigaverse.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (32).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Juice Vendor</p></figcaption></figure>

1. Click on the Juice Vending machine located in the spawn area.
2. Select the duration of Giga Juice you'd like to purchase, and complete the checkout flow.
3. Once you are juiced, you will receive all the [benefits-of-giga-juice.md](benefits-of-giga-juice.md "mention").
