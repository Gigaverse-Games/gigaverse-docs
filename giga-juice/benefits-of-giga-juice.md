# Benefits of Giga Juice

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/juiceb.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption><p>Get Juiced</p></figcaption></figure>

Giga Juice is an important in-game power-up that has several benefits as listed below. It can be purchased in 4 different durations: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or 180 days. It is more economical (i.e. cheaper on a daily basis) to purchase the longer subscriptions. Our most committed players stay juiced.\
\
Current benefits of Giga Juice:&#x20;

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/enenrgy.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Energy

* Increased daily limit -> <mark style="color:red;">240</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">420</mark>
* Faster regeneration -> <mark style="color:red;">10/hour</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">17.5/hour</mark>

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/gigarom.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Gigaverse ROM

* Material (Shard & Dust) Production boost -> <mark style="color:red;">0%</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">20%</mark>

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/noob.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Giga Noob

* Extra potion slot -> <mark style="color:red;">2</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">3</mark>
* Movement -> <mark style="color:red;">Walk</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">Sprint</mark> (press Shift)

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/juicechest.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Premium Loot

* Exclusive chest containing trinkets -> <mark style="color:green;">Yes</mark>

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/swrd.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Dungeon&#x20;

* High intensity runs -> <mark style="color:red;">1x</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">3x</mark> (energy cost and loot drops)
* Extra upgrade option during runs -> <mark style="color:red;">3 option</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">4 options</mark> (50% chance)
* Extra runs per day
  * Dungetron 5000 -> <mark style="color:red;">10/day</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">12/day</mark>&#x20;
  * Dungetron Underhaul -> <mark style="color:red;">8/day</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">9/day</mark>

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/fishinnicon.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Fishing

* Extra casts (runs) per day -> <mark style="color:red;">10/day</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">20/day</mark>

### <img src="../.gitbook/assets/abs.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Abstract Eco

* Bonus Abstract Stubs conversion rate at Traveling Merchant -> <mark style="color:red;">1x</mark> to <mark style="color:green;">x4</mark>

### Extra Rewards

Extra Rewards can be part of Giga Juice purchase. These are not always available and will be explicitly mentioned at the Giga Juice Vending machine purchase section.

{% hint style="success" %}
Extra Rewards are occasional and often for limited time only.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
