# Echo Battles

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Echoes are powerful enemies based on other player's stats.&#x20;

They:

* have a chance to appear in every room with levels close to yours
* have exact hp & mana as the other player in that room
* show other player name, level, faction & vanity skin
* they can drop useful event items during specific competitions

Both Dungetron 5000 and Dungetron Underhaul can spawn echoes.

They will only appear after reaching level 10 in each dungeon.
