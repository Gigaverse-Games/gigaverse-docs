# Dungetron 5000: Gigus

The Gigus Dungeon (named after [gigus-maximus.md](../../lore-and-special-characters/gigus-maximus.md "mention")) is an extreme version of the dungeon with a high energy entry cost. It does not drop any scrap, but is the only place in gigaverse where you can earn gigus materials.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/dungeon.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Combat Mechanic

Combat works in the same way as in the Normal dungeon.&#x20;

### Entry Requirement

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Asset_Icon_Energy 1.png" alt="" data-size="line">200 energy must be spent to enter the Gigus dungeon.

### Rewards

Gigus materials are found in the Gigus dungeon. This is the only place in the game they can be found.&#x20;

### Daily Limit

30 runs per day (this is subject to change in the future).

### Flee

You are able to flee the dungeon (end your run without dying) by approaching the ladder once you've defeated an enemy, and before progressing to the next room.

### Consumables

You can craft or purchase consumables to take in to the dungeon with you.&#x20;
