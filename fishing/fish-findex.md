# Fish Findex

### Findex

Records of your fishing career.

Shows how many fish you’ve caught of each type, including higher quality fish of that type

Shows your personal best weight, length, and girth of each fish type caught

Once you catch a fish, it will appear in the Findex along with its unique stats and rarity.

### Quality and Mastery Stars

Each fish has a quality level, starting at 1-Star

Upon catching a 2-Star or higher fish, earn 40-60% bonus seaweed of that fish’s base sell value instantly per star past 1

{% hint style="success" %}
Hint: You can encounter higher quality fish starting at lvl 10 fishing, or by upgrading the Taste skill
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To complete a Mastery Star for a Fish, you need to catch 10 fish of that quality

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For example: Catch ten 1-Star Glub Glubs to get the 1st Glub Glub Mastery Star. For the 2nd Mastery Star, you would need to catch ten 2-Star Glub Glubs.

{% hint style="success" %}
Mastery Stars are currently only for collecting/record purposes
{% endhint %}
