# Fish Stall

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Fish Stall</p></figcaption></figure>

Tonno is the main vendor for everything fishing related

Chat with Tonno at the Fishing Stall to:

* Sell fish for Seaweed
* Upgrade your Skills with seaweed
* Search your Findex
* View your Fishing Equipment and Daily/Rod Spells
* Examine Catch Rates
* Check for ongoing Fishing Competitions
