# Overview

Crafting is the only way to acquire advanced items, consumables and more.

These advanced items provide various perks in-game.&#x20;

### Crafting Requirements

* Crafting Station
* Receipe
* Materials
* Energy

### What are Materials?

Materials are the base items required to craft.

General materials can be found in the dungeons. Higher floors & rooms have rarer drops.

Faction specific material can only be claimed via ROMs.

Check out [materials.md](materials.md "mention")for more information.
